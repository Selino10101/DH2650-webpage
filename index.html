About Notes Of Freedom

The animals lived peacefully in the woods until a mad scientist moved into the forest, then suddenly they started to disappear one by one. One day they wake up in a strange metal box, walking on two legs. The scientist explains that he has given them the power of humans, and in return they shall work for him endlessly. Indeed they try to escape. But on the way they find that the only way to open the doors of the castle is to play musical devices set up in each room.

Notes of freedom is a 2D-platformer where your goal is to guide a ball into musical notes. To unlock the next stage and escape the mad scientist, you have to hit all of the notes in one swoop.
